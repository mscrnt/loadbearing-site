§02

The pitch

A near-future where aesthetics overpowered engineering rigor — first in buildings, then in politics. The Architects' Guild controls the codes; public works are failing. You're the last licensed Civil Engineer (re-certified in secret by an underground order), working from a cluttered basement in the city of Port Moment.

Each level is a timed city: a set of issues, a clock, and a hammer. Fix every issue before the timer runs out, complete the city, advance to a bigger and more broken one. The job never ends. That's the joke.

§03

Systems overview

01

The hammer

One cursor. One tool. Every swing lands an impact on the targeted issue. Multi-target / AoE / crit / charge-up unlock directly on the hammer instead of swapping tools.

02

Apprentices

Autonomous units that fix issues alongside you. The apprentices are the in-game auto-clicker — more of them, faster of them. Idle assists that earn while you triage.

03

Three currencies

Specs earned from hammering, spent in-run. Permits earned by completing a city, spent on permanent unlocks. PE Stamps — prestige, banked on "Submit for Peer Review."

04

Skill tree

Permits buy permanent nodes: click power, AoE, generator multipliers, between-city accrual. Branches + capstones differentiate styles — the named disciplines are still firming up in development.

05

The Architects on the roadmap

Each high-profile antagonist is a parody of a real architecture movement; their boss fights are design reviews. Designed; not yet in the build — landing during Early Access alongside their art pass.

06

Submit for Peer Review

The prestige loop. Reset the run to permanent multipliers — your PE Stamps compound across runs. The bigger the citation, the bigger the next collapse.
